# Official Syllabus Transcription: School Assistant (Mathematics)

## Examination Pattern
- **Total Marks:** 80 Marks
- **Total Questions:** 160 Questions (Official Scheme: 0.5 marks per question)
- **Practice Test Model:** 80 Questions (1 mark per question, 80 marks total)
- **Duration:** 150 Minutes (2 Hours 30 Minutes)

---

## Part - I: General Knowledge and Current Affairs (10 Marks / 20 Questions)
1. Current events of National, International, and Regional importance.
2. Indian Polity and Governance: Constitution, Political System, Panchayati Raj, Public Policy, Rights Issues.
3. Indian History and National Movement.
4. Physical, Social, and Economic Geography of India.
5. Economic and Social Development: Sustainable Development, Poverty, Inclusion, Demographics, Social Sector Initiatives.
6. General Science and Technology: Everyday scientific phenomena, recent space, defense, and digital innovations in India.
7. Environmental Ecology, Biodiversity, Climate Change, Disaster Management.
8. Telangana / Andhra Pradesh State Policies, Heritage, Culture, Literature, and Flagship Development Schemes.

## Part - II: Perspectives in Education (10 Marks / 20 Questions)
1. **History of Education in India:**
   - Pre-Independent India: Indigenous Education, Charter Act of 1813, Macaulay's Minute (1835), Wood's Despatch (1854), Hunter Commission (1882), Hartog Committee (1929), Wardha Scheme of Basic Education (1937), Sargent Plan (1944).
   - Post-Independent India: University Education Commission (Radhakrishnan Commission, 1948-49), Secondary Education Commission (Mudaliar Commission, 1952-53), Indian Education Commission (Kothari Commission, 1964-66), National Policy on Education (NPE, 1986), Programme of Action (POA, 1992), National Education Policy (NEP, 2020).
2. **Teacher Empowerment & Professional Ethics:**
   - Professional Code of Conduct for Teachers, Professional Development, Self-evaluation, Teacher Motivation, Accountability.
   - Institutional Roles: NCERT, NCTE, NIEPA, SCERT, DIET, IASE, CTE.
3. **Contemporary Educational Concerns & Inclusive Education:**
   - Environmental Education and Sustainable Development.
   - Population Education and Family Life Education.
   - Gender Equality, Girl Child Education, Inclusive Education, Children with Special Needs (CWSN).
   - Educational equity, Human Resource Development, Lifelong Learning.
4. **Acts, Rights, and Curriculum Frameworks:**
   - Children's Rights: UNCRC, Protection of Child Rights Act 2005, POCSO Act 2012.
   - Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act, 2009 (Rules, Provisions, and Implementation).
   - Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005.
   - National Curriculum Framework (NCF) 2005 & State Curriculum Frameworks (APSCF/TSCF).
   - National Educational Programmes: SSA, RMSA, Samagra Shiksha, Mid-Day Meal / PM POSHAN, KGBV, Model Schools.
   - School Curriculum, Evaluation reforms, Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE).

## Part - III: Content — Mathematics (44 Marks / 88 Questions)
1. **Number Systems:**
   - Natural numbers, Whole numbers, Integers, Rational numbers, Irrational numbers, Real numbers.
   - Divisibility rules, Prime and Composite numbers, Coprimes, Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic.
   - LCM and HCF, Euclid's Division Lemma, Prime factorization.
   - Representation of real numbers on the number line, Terminating and non-terminating recurring decimals.
   - Laws of Exponents, Surds (Radicals), Operations on surds, Rationalisation of surds.
   - Logarithms: Definition, Laws of Logarithms, Natural and Common logarithms, Applications.
   - Complex Numbers: Imaginary unit i, Modulus, Conjugate, Polar representation, Argand plane, Fundamental operations.
2. **Sets, Relations, and Functions:**
   - Sets and their representations, Empty set, Finite and Infinite sets, Subsets, Power set, Universal set.
   - Venn diagrams, Union and Intersection of sets, Difference of sets, Complement of a set, De Morgan's Laws.
   - Cartesian product of sets, Relations, Domain, Co-domain, Range, Equivalence relations.
   - Functions: Types of functions (One-one, Onto, Bijective), Composite functions, Inverse functions.
3. **Commercial Mathematics:**
   - Ratio and Proportion, Direct and Inverse proportion, Compounded ratio.
   - Percentages, Profit and Loss, Discount, Marked price.
   - Simple Interest and Compound Interest (compounded annually, half-yearly, continuously).
   - Time and Work, Unitary method, Pipes and Cisterns.
   - Time, Speed, and Distance, Relative speed, Problems on Trains, Boats and Streams.
   - Partnership, Ratio of profit sharing.
4. **Linear Equations and Algebra:**
   - Polynomials: Zeros of a polynomial, Division algorithm for polynomials, Remainder and Factor theorems.
   - Algebraic identities, Factorisation of polynomials.
   - Linear equations in one and two variables, Graphical and Algebraic solutions (Substitution, Elimination, Cross-multiplication).
   - Consistency and Inconsistency of systems of linear equations.
   - Quadratic Equations: Standard form, Solution by factorisation, Completing the square, Quadratic formula.
   - Discriminant and Nature of roots, Relation between roots and coefficients, Equations reducible to quadratic equations.
   - Mathematical Induction: Principle of Mathematical Induction and applications.
   - Binomial Theorem: Binomial theorem for positive integral index, General and middle terms, Properties of binomial coefficients.
5. **Progressions:**
   - Arithmetic Progression (AP): Definition, nth term, Sum of first n terms, Arithmetic Mean.
   - Geometric Progression (GP): Definition, nth term, Sum of first n terms, Sum to infinity, Geometric Mean.
   - Harmonic Progression (HP): Definition, Relation between AM, GM, and HM.
6. **Geometry:**
   - Euclidean Geometry: Axioms, Postulates, Definitions.
   - Lines and Angles: Intersecting and Parallel lines, Transversals, Alternate and Corresponding angles.
   - Triangles: Types, Properties, Angle sum property, Exterior angle property.
   - Congruence of Triangles (SAS, ASA, AAS, SSS, RHS).
   - Similarity of Triangles (AAA, SAS, SSS criteria), Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales Theorem) and its converse.
   - Pythagoras Theorem, Converse of Pythagoras Theorem, Apollonius Theorem.
   - Quadrilaterals: Properties of Parallelograms, Rectangles, Rhombuses, Squares, Trapeziums, Kites.
   - Circles: Chords, Tangents, Secants, Cyclic quadrilaterals, Tangent-secant theorems, Alternate segment theorem.
   - Geometric Constructions: Bisectors, Triangles, Tangents to circles.
7. **Mensuration:**
   - Perimeter and Area of Plane Figures: Triangles (Heron's formula), Quadrilaterals, Circles, Sectors, Segments.
   - Surface Area and Volume of Solid Figures: Cubes, Cuboids, Right Circular Cylinders, Cones, Spheres, Hemispheres, Frustum of a cone.
   - Combination and Conversion of solids.
8. **Trigonometry:**
   - Trigonometric ratios of acute angles, Trigonometric ratios of specific angles (0, 30, 45, 60, 90 degrees).
   - Trigonometric ratios of complementary angles, Fundamental Trigonometric identities.
   - Heights and Distances: Angle of elevation, Angle of depression, Multi-step practical problems.
   - Compound angles, Multiple and Sub-multiple angles, Transformation formulas.
   - Properties of Triangles: Sine rule, Cosine rule, Projection rule, Tangent rule (Napier's analogy), Area of a triangle.
   - Inverse Trigonometric Functions: Domain, Range, Principal value branch, Properties.
9. **Analytical and Coordinate Geometry:**
   - Distance between two points, Section formula (Internal and External), Midpoint formula, Centroid of a triangle.
   - Area of a triangle using coordinates, Collinearity of three points.
   - Straight Lines: Slope of a line, Various forms of equations of a line (Slope-intercept, Point-slope, Two-point, Intercept, Normal form).
   - Angle between two lines, Distance of a point from a line, Distance between parallel lines.
   - Conic Sections: Circle (Standard and General equations), Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola (Standard equations, Focus, Directrix, Eccentricity, Latus Rectum).
10. **Matrices and Determinants:**
    - Types of Matrices, Operations on matrices (Addition, Scalar multiplication, Matrix multiplication).
    - Transpose of a matrix, Symmetric and Skew-symmetric matrices.
    - Determinants of matrices (up to order 3x3), Minors, Cofactors, Properties of determinants.
    - Adjoint and Inverse of a square matrix.
    - Solving systems of linear equations using Cramer's Rule, Matrix Inversion Method, and Gauss Elimination.
11. **Statistics and Probability:**
    - Collection, Organisation, and Presentation of Data: Frequency distribution tables, Histograms, Frequency polygons, Ogives.
    - Measures of Central Tendency: Mean (Direct, Assumed Mean, Step-deviation), Median, Mode of grouped and ungrouped data.
    - Measures of Dispersion: Range, Mean Deviation, Variance, Standard Deviation.
    - Permutations and Combinations: Fundamental principle of counting, Factorial notation, nPr, nCr, Applications.
    - Probability: Classical definition, Empirical definition, Addition theorem of probability.
    - Conditional probability, Independent events, Bayes' Theorem, Random variables, Binomial distribution.
12. **Computing Fundamentals, Algorithms, and Flowcharts:**
    - Fundamentals of Computers: Hardware, Software, Input/Output devices, Memory hierarchy.
    - Number Systems in Computing: Binary, Octal, Decimal, Hexadecimal representation and conversions.
    - Algorithms: Definition, Characteristics, Step-by-step logic, Pseudo-code.
    - Flowcharts: Standard flowchart symbols, Sequence, Selection (decision making), Iteration (looping) constructs.

## Part - IV: Teaching Methodology / Pedagogy of Mathematics (16 Marks / 32 Questions)
1. **Meaning, Nature, and Scope of Mathematics:**
   - Definitions of Mathematics, Nature (Logical, Abstract, Precise, Order, Pattern, Symbolic language).
   - Historical perspectives and Contributions of Mathematicians: Aryabhata, Brahmagupta, Bhaskaracharya, Srinivasa Ramanujan, Euclid, Pythagoras, Rene Descartes, Carl Friedrich Gauss, George Cantor.
2. **Aims and Values of Teaching Mathematics:**
   - Utilitarian / Practical value, Disciplinary / Intellectual value, Cultural value, Aesthetic value, Social value, Vocational value.
   - Instructional Objectives and Specifications: Bloom's Taxonomy of Educational Objectives (Cognitive, Affective, Psychomotor domains), Anderson & Krathwohl revised taxonomy.
   - Formulating Learning Outcomes and Behavioral Objectives in Mathematics.
3. **Methods and Approaches of Teaching Mathematics:**
   - Inductive Method and Deductive Method (Merits, Demerits, Suitability).
   - Analytic Method and Synthetic Method.
   - Heuristic Method (Armstrong's inquiry method).
   - Problem Solving Method (Steps of problem solving, Polya's heuristic).
   - Project Method (Kilpatrick's purposeful activity).
   - Laboratory Method (Learning by doing, Activity-based mathematics).
4. **Planning for Instruction:**
   - Annual / Year Plan: Need, Structure, Components.
   - Unit Plan: Structure, Learning experiences, Sub-units.
   - Lesson Plan: Need, Herbartian steps (Preparation, Presentation, Association, Generalisation, Application, Recapitulation), Constructivist / 5E Model (Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate).
5. **Learning Resources and Mathematics Laboratory:**
   - Edgar Dale's Cone of Experience and its implications for Mathematics education.
   - Teaching-Learning Materials (TLMs): Concrete manipulatives, Low-cost and No-cost TLMs, Digital learning tools, GeoGebra.
   - Mathematics Textbook: Criteria of a good textbook (Vogel's spot check, Hunter score card), Evaluation of textbooks.
   - Mathematics Library and Mathematics Club: Objectives, Organization, Activities, Recreational mathematics, Math Olympiads.
   - Mathematics Laboratory: Equipment, Worksheets, Organization, Experiential learning.
6. **Catring to Diverse Learners:**
   - Teaching gifted children, creative children, and mathematically slow learners / underachievers.
   - Dyscalculia and mathematical learning difficulties: Identification, Diagnostic tests, Remedial teaching strategies.
   - Oral work, Written work, Drill work, Speed and Accuracy, Home assignments.
7. **Assessment and Evaluation in Mathematics:**
   - Concept of Measurement, Assessment, and Evaluation.
   - Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE): Formative Assessment (Tools, Weightages) and Summative Assessment.
   - Types of Tests: Teacher-made tests vs. Standardized tests, Diagnostic tests, Achievement tests.
   - Test Construction: Blueprints, Weightage tables, Item writing, Scoring key and Marking scheme.
   - Characteristics of a Good Test: Validity, Reliability, Objectivity, Usability.
